# Fellowship Budget

The budget below is a planning framework, not a claim about an awarded grant.

## First $400K

| Category | Planned amount | Purpose |
|---|---:|---|
| Founder runway | $160K | Full-time execution during research and validation |
| Technical collaborator / co-founder search | $80K | Contracted technical support and transition toward a founding team |
| Compute and experiment infrastructure | $60K | Controlled model/SAE experiments; scale only after validation |
| Customer discovery / travel / pilots | $40K | Design-partner discovery after technical gates |
| Legal, incorporation, IP and security | $30K | Company formation and foundational legal/security work |
| Operating reserve | $30K | Buffer for iteration and unexpected costs |
| **Total** | **$400K** | |

## Follow-on $600K

The $600K follow-on should be treated as an external-round support mechanism rather than assumed operating cash today. Its purpose is to strengthen the next venture round after technical and customer evidence exists.

## Credits / perks

SPC's current Fall 2026 page states up to $1M in credits and perks. Compute consumption should therefore be milestone-driven. The project should not imply that the entire headline credit amount will necessarily be consumed.

## Capital discipline

The sequencing principle is:

**measure → validate → scale**

rather than:

**spend → assume → rationalize**
