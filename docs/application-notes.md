## Evidence discipline

The original narrative contains several statements that should not be repeated as facts without direct evidence. These include:

- a claimed 50% infrastructure reduction from Group-SAE;
- a claimed production latency below 50 ms;
- claims that SAE-NO will materially stabilize representations;
- claims that latent steering alone can surgically block PII extraction or malicious code;
- detailed predictions about SPC resident counts, internal mentoring ratios, or specific budget entitlements beyond what SPC currently publishes.

These are now framed as hypotheses, targets, or program facts only where current SPC materials support them.

## Competitive positioning

Goodfire is a directly relevant SPC company and a major validation that mechanistic interpretability can become a venture-scale category. Goodfire announced a $150M Series B in February 2026 at a $1.25B valuation. NeuroSteer should therefore avoid presenting itself as simply “another interpretability company.” The differentiation must be the specific inference-time control problem, the open-weight deployment focus, and measurable systems economics.

## Strategic positioning

The strongest SPC framing is not “I already have a finished company.” It is:

> I have a technically concrete thesis, an initial reproducible research artifact, explicit falsification criteria, and a clear reason why SPC's technical community can help determine the highest-value version of the opportunity.
