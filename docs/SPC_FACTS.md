# SPC Facts Used in the Application

Source: official South Park Commons Founder Fellowship Fall 2026 page, checked 2026-09-05.

- Founder Fellowship Fall 2026 was announced July 8, 2026.
- Published application deadline: 11:59pm PT, Sunday, August 2, 2026.
- Published funding terms: $400K upfront for 7% via a standard SAFE, plus $600K guaranteed in the next external funding round.
- Published credits/perks: up to $1M.
- Published program structure: immediate funding/membership on acceptance, followed by an eight-week in-person bootcamp and then residency with no fixed end date.
- Published solo-founder policy: solo founders can apply; SPC states that founders who would benefit from a co-founder can search within the SPC community.
- Published locations for the Fall 2026 bootcamp: San Francisco, New York City, or Bangalore.

Application consequence:

Do not state that the Fall 2026 application is currently open. The published deadline has passed. The application document can be used as a revised application package, future cohort package, or direct outreach material, but submission status must be verified separately at the time of sending.
